# ✍️ In-Class Argumentative Writing Task

## 📌 Context
In **Critical Thinking and English Writing II**, we have studied:
- What argumentation is  
- The **Toulmin model** (claim, reasons, evidence, warrant)  
- **Counter-arguments** and **rebuttals**

This in-class task gives you the opportunity to **apply these skills** by writing a **complete argumentative essay** within a limited time.

---

## 🧠 Writing Topic

> **Should generative AI tools (such as Deepseek, Doubao, ChatGPT) be allowed in university writing courses?**

---

## 📝 Task Requirements

Write a **complete argumentative essay** that includes all of the following components:

### 1️⃣ Position
- Clearly state **your position** in the introduction  
- Your stance should be **explicit and arguable** (not neutral)

### 2️⃣ Supporting Arguments
- Present **at least two arguments** supporting your position  
- Each argument should include:
  - A clear **claim**
  - **Reasons** and/or **evidence** (examples, observations, common experiences, or general facts)

### 3️⃣ Counter-Argument & Rebuttal
- Identify **at least one reasonable opposing view**
- Show that you **understand** why some people disagree with you
- Respond to the counter-argument by:
  - Explaining its limitations, **or**
  - Showing why your position is stronger or more practical

### 4️⃣  Conclusion
- Restate your position briefly
- Highlight why your argument matters for **college learning and writing**

---

## ⏱️ Time & Length
- **Time:** 90 mins  
- **Length:** Approximately **500 words**

---


## 💡 Reminder
This task is not about giving the “right” answer.  
It is about showing **clear reasoning**, **critical engagement**, and **structured argumentation**.

Write thoughtfully, argue fairly, and use examples that make sense.
